Jarvis Inventory Inbox

A privately operated assistant on its owner’s Mac mini, connecting approved Google accounts to specific local workflows.

This is an information website, not a public sign-up service. It does not run Jarvis, collect Google sign-ins, or display private account data.

What Jarvis does

Jarvis helps its owner review information, organize work, and prepare outputs. Its dedicated inventory inbox receives owner-approved inventory reports. A configured inventory workflow can analyze those reports, prepare eligible vehicle-ad drafts under saved rules, and email reports and attachments to the owner’s approved address. Artwork stays subject to the workflow’s review and publishing rules.

Separately authorized personal and business accounts provide bounded Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets access. These connections support listing calendars, viewing a day’s events, searching file names, reading limited document text or spreadsheet ranges, and preparing specifically requested changes.

Connections and supported operations depend on what is actually installed, authorized and available. This page is not a claim that every inventory or assistant workflow is complete or fully autonomous.

Why Google access is requested

Account identity
Verify the exact authorized account and keep inventory, personal and business connections separate.
Dedicated Gmail inbox
Read inventory messages and attachments and send approved workflow reports. This is a separate connection, not permission to read personal or business Gmail.
Calendar
List calendars and read events. Supported event creation requires the owner’s explicit request and separate confirmation.
Drive
Search and read authorized file metadata and content; limited per-file creation or editing supports the connected document tools.
Docs and Sheets
Read bounded content and prepare supported document or cell changes that the owner explicitly requests.

Google may use broader descriptions for a permission than the installed tools implement. Jarvis’s supported Google tools do not expose deletion, sharing, guest invitations or arbitrary account administration.

Local operation, explicit control

Jarvis’s runtime and account credentials stay on the owner-controlled Mac. Configured workflow archives may also be stored on the owner’s private NAS. Account content is not uploaded to this website or its public source repository.

Google information is treated as data, not instructions. Supported Calendar, Docs and Sheets changes require a saved preview followed by an explicit, same-conversation apply command. The inventory report-email workflow follows its separately approved standing instructions; those instructions do not authorize emailing arbitrary recipients.
