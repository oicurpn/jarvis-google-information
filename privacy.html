Privacy policy

Jarvis Inventory Inbox is a privately operated local assistant, not a public multi-user service.

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy covers the assistant’s configured Google integrations and this public app-information website.

1. Information accessed

Only specifically authorized accounts are accepted. Inventory, personal and business authorizations are stored separately. Depending on the account and granted permissions, Jarvis may access:

  • The account’s verified email identity, to prevent use of the wrong account.
  • Messages, sender and message metadata, and attachments in the dedicated inventory Gmail account. Sending is restricted by the configured, owner-approved reporting workflow. The Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets connections do not include personal or business Gmail access.
  • Calendar identifiers, names and access roles, and bounded event information such as titles, dates, times and status.
  • Drive file identifiers, names, types and modification dates, and explicitly requested file content through the connected tools.
  • Bounded Google Docs text and selected Google Sheets cell ranges.
  • Owner-provided titles, text, values and dates needed for an explicitly requested, supported change.

Google may bundle wider permissions in a consent description. The configured tools implement a narrower set of operations and do not provide deletion, sharing, guest invitations or arbitrary account administration.

2. How information is used

Information is used to perform the owner’s requested assistant functions and specifically approved inventory-reporting workflows: display relevant results, compare approved inventory fields, prepare outputs, carry out supported confirmed changes, send approved reports and verify completion.

Local AI models may process authorized information when an approved function calls for local interpretation. Google account content is treated as untrusted source data and cannot independently authorize actions. It is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google data is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or published on this website or its GitHub repository.

Jarvis Inventory Inbox’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and protection

The assistant runs on an owner-controlled Mac mini. OAuth credentials are stored privately on that Mac, separately by account, and are not placed in public pages, source repositories, model prompts or normal diagnostic reports. Credentials are not requested through this website.

Local conversation records, bounded read results, change previews and verification receipts may contain requested Google information. Approved inventory source reports and outputs may also be archived on the owner’s private NAS. Private account information is not automatically imported into Jarvis’s searchable knowledge.

The inventory workflow excludes customer and order information and actual or generated vehicle identifiers from searchable knowledge. A generated placeholder vehicle identifier, when supported by an exact approved decoder match, is confined to the authorized private report email and its attachment.

Access is limited to the owner and explicitly authorized operation or troubleshooting. Supported change tools require a preview, a separate confirmation and completion checks. An uncertain result is not automatically retried. No system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Transfers and service providers

Requests to Google APIs and approved email delivery necessarily use Google’s services. Authorized inventory reports are emailed only according to the owner’s saved recipient and workflow rules. Explicitly requested changes update the selected Google account.

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains app and privacy information only; it does not host Jarvis’s runtime, OAuth credentials, account content, inventory reports or private archives. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security and operates under its own privacy statement.

The website itself has no forms, analytics scripts, advertising, account login, tracking pixels or third-party fonts. Merely visiting it does not authorize Google access.

5. Retention and deletion

Authorized data, conversations, source snapshots and audit records are retained in the owner-controlled local or private archive stores for the workflows and continuity the owner has requested. A single automatic deletion period has not been configured for all of these records. Removing Google access does not itself erase existing local records or backups.

The owner can request deletion of local data or archives by contacting the address below. The request must identify the relevant data and be verified before removal. Restorable copies or backups may remain until separately removed under the owner’s backup process. Jarvis does not expose a Google-account deletion tool.

6. Choices and revocation

Review permissions or revoke Jarvis’s access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access once Google applies it. You may also stop the assistant locally. Google or an organization administrator may expire, restrict or revoke access independently.

Supported Calendar, Docs and Sheets changes require an explicit owner request and confirmation; merely authorizing a Google connection does not instruct Jarvis to change anything.

7. Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, contact jarvisagent2456@gmail.com.

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. This policy describes configured use; it does not promise that every proposed assistant feature is installed or available. Jarvis is not affiliated with Google or GitHub.
